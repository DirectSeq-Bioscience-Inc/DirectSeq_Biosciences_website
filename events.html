UAlbany Researchers Advance New Technique for Direct RNA Sequencing

University at Albany's Department of Chemistry features DirectSeq Biosciences founder and associate professor Shenglong Zhang and his team's latest breakthrough — 3D NGMS-Seq, a three-dimensional next-generation mass spectrometry sequencing platform published in Nucleic Acids Research. The article highlights how the technique directly sequences RNA, including all chemical modifications, without prior sequence input — uncovering hidden impurities and isomers in therapeutic RNA samples that conventional methods miss entirely.
